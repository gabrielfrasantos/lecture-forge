## Title

Energy and Co-energy | Force and Torque from Flux Linkage — PMSM & BLDC Lesson 8

## Description

```
How do you find the force on a piece of steel that carries no current? This lesson derives force and torque from energy: the energy balance of a lossless coupling field (i dλ = dW_f + F dx), stored field energy W_f = ∫ i dλ and co-energy W′ = ∫ λ di as the two areas on the λ–i curve, and why F = −∂W_f/∂x at constant λ equals +∂W′/∂x at constant i. A reluctance actuator (U-core, 500 turns, moving armature) is worked through with numbers: the linear result F = ½ i² dL/dx, a 2D finite-difference field solution whose co-energy force agrees with the Maxwell stress tensor within 0.2 %, and the saturated case with Lesson 4's steel, where ½ i² dL/dx no longer applies. Then torque T = ∂W′/∂θ, several windings and a magnet (setting up Lesson 9), and a bench exercise with a kitchen scale and an LCR meter.

Every figure is tagged on screen as calculated, analytic, schematic or historical reconstruction.
```
