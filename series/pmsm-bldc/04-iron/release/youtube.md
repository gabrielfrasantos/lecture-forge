## Title

Iron: Saturation and Losses | B–H Curves, Hysteresis, Eddy Currents — PMSM & BLDC Lesson 4

## Description

```
Why is a motor's stator a stack of thin, insulated steel sheets? This lesson builds the answer from typical data for 0.35 mm non-oriented electrical steel (M270-35A class): magnetic domains and saturation, the B–H curve and how permeability collapses above the knee near 1.5 T, what saturation does to the magnetic circuit of Lesson 3, the hysteresis loop (Jiles–Atherton model) and why its area is energy per cycle, eddy currents and the classical lamination formula P = π²t²f²B²/6ρ, a fitted loss-separation model, the stator frequency and iron loss of the series' reference motor, and a bench B–H loop tracer with an RC integrator.

Every figure is tagged on screen as calculated, analytic, schematic or historical reconstruction.
```
