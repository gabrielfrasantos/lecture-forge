## Title

Magnetic Circuits: Why the Air Gap Sets a Motor's Field | PMSM & BLDC — Lesson 3

## Description

```
Why does half a millimetre of air, not centimetres of steel, set the field in a permanent-magnet motor? This lesson builds the magnetic circuit from Ampère's law: MMF, flux and reluctance, a gapped C-core computed step by step, the equivalent air length of steel (l/μr), and where the circuit analogy breaks (leakage and fringing, shown with a 2D finite-difference field solution). Then the permanent magnet: the second-quadrant recoil line, the load line and permeance coefficient, and the air-gap flux density of the series' 12-slot / 8-pole reference motor (≈ 0.94 T before leakage and slotting). Bench exercise: predict the gap field of an NdFeB magnet in a steel yoke and check it with a Hall-probe gaussmeter.

Every figure is tagged on screen as calculated, analytic, schematic or historical reconstruction.
```
