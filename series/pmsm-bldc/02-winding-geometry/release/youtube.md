## Title

Winding Geometry | Slots, the MMF Staircase and the Winding Function — PMSM & BLDC Lesson 2

## Description

```
How do coils packed into slots make the rotating field of a PMSM or BLDC motor? This lesson applies Ampère's law across the air gap, with ideal iron, to turn a winding table into the air-gap MMF staircase: one coil, one phase, then all three phases at an instant and as time runs. It introduces the turns and winding functions and computes the Fourier content of the 12-slot / 8-pole concentrated winding of the series' reference motor (q = 1/2, winding factor 0.866, its harmonics, and why it has no sub-harmonics, unlike 12 slots / 10 poles).

Exercises: script the MMF yourself (the code is shown and checked on screen), and measure phase resistance with a four-wire (Kelvin) connection and copper's temperature correction. The model's limits (ideal iron, slot openings ignored, no saturation) are stated and deferred to later lessons.

Every figure is tagged on screen as calculated, analytic, schematic or historical reconstruction.
```
